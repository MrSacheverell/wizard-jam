## Visual effects
Explosion, Frost Nova, Ice Shatter, Zap Hit, Blink In/Out and Hit Spark effects
are rendered from **pixelfx** by Patch — https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx
MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Patch, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez.
Some pixelfx effects adapt Sprite Fusion's open-source pixel art VFX
(https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez.

Rendered with `node tools/render.mjs <id> --sheet --no-actor --fps 20`, then trimmed and cropped.
Explosion palettes are pixelfx defaults; Zap Hit is recolored yellow (Shock) and Hit Spark violet (Arcane).
